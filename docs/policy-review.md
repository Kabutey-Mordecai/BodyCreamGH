## Privacy Policy
*How we protect your personal information under the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).*

### 1. Information We Collect
BodyCream GH collects only the details needed to process your orders and improve your shopping experience: your name, email address, phone number, delivery address (including optional GhanaPostGPS code and landmark), and order history. Card and Mobile Money details are processed securely by Paystack and never stored on our servers.

### 2. How We Use Your Data
We use your contact and delivery information to fulfill orders, send transactional order and dispatch updates, respond to support requests, and prevent fraud. You may unsubscribe from promotional emails at any time via the unsubscribe link in any newsletter.

### 3. Data Sharing & Retention
We share delivery details only with vetted dispatch partners and payment verification details with Paystack. We do not sell or rent customer personal data to third parties.

### 4. Your Rights & Contact
You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at hello@bodycreamgh.com or +233 24 585 2804.

## Refund & Return Policy
*14-day return guarantee on eligible unopened skincare items.*

### 1. Return Eligibility
You may request a return within 14 days of delivery if you received a wrong, damaged, or defective item, or if the product remains unopened, unused, and in its original sealed packaging.

### 2. Hygiene & Cosmetic Exemptions
For health and hygiene protection, opened, unsealed, or used skincare jars, bottles, and scrubs cannot be returned unless the item arrived damaged or defective.

### 3. Refund Processing
Once a return is approved and inspected, Paystack refunds are initiated to your original Mobile Money wallet or card (typically 3–7 business days depending on your provider). Pay-on-Delivery orders are refunded via Mobile Money.

## Shipping & Delivery Policy
*Fast doorstep delivery across Accra and nationwide across Ghana.*

### 1. Delivery Fees & Free Delivery
Greater Accra delivery is GHS 25.00 and Nationwide delivery is GHS 50.00. Orders over GHS 200.00 qualify for FREE standard Accra delivery.

### 2. Estimated Delivery Windows
Greater Accra orders typically arrive within 1–2 business days. Regional / Nationwide deliveries arrive within 3–5 business days. Providing a GhanaPostGPS code and clear landmark helps riders deliver faster.

### 3. Store Pickup
Free pickup is available at East Legon, Accra (Call upon arrival) (Mon–Sat, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM). Wait for your "Ready for Pickup" confirmation before visiting.
